# Daily Eating Structure

*Baseline protocol — food structure first, clock-agnostic. For time windows and lifestyle rules, see [[Meridian Clock (Solar-Adjusted)]].*

## Core Structure (Overview)

1. **Breakfast opener**
   - 2 eggs
   - Protein smoothie (split into two servings: half now, half 60–90 min later or with lunch)
   - Optional digestion add-in: **1 tbsp chia (soaked)** or **1 tbsp ground flax**

2. **Morning graze**
   - Nutty pudding
   - Green tea preferred over coffee

3. **Main lunch block**
   - Lentils (½ cup dry, cooked) + olive oil
   - Quinoa (¼ cup dry, cooked) + olive oil
   - Cauliflower/broccoli + olive oil

4. **Final meal**
   - Curry sauce + sweet potato (calorie dial)

5. **Evening close**
   - No food after final meal window closes

## Digestion Rules (current baseline)
- Avoid one giant morning bolus where possible (split smoothie/supplements)
- Add one fiber lever at a time (chia **or** flax **or** psyllium)
- Hydrate more when using chia/flax/psyllium
- Change one variable for 4–7 days before judging

## PHS Note — Consecutive Eating
- Keep meals simple; eat one main item at a time where practical
- Sequence over complexity
